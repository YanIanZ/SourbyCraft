## What is in Build 53 — co-op play, authoritative login, adapter contract (COOP-DESIGN slice 5 + RFC E03)

MultiProfile and co-op stay **off by default**. With MultiProfile disabled, Build 53 behaves like Build 52,
including the shutdown order. With co-op disabled, `login.authoritative: auto` keeps solo MultiProfile on Build 52
behaviour: the join loads vanilla playerdata. One difference applies to everyone using MultiProfile: shutdown now
writes quit snapshots (see below).

- **Co-op members can play the co-op profile.**
  - Switching in and out uses per-member snapshot streams, with capture tickets bound to the session generation.
    A stale A→B→A capture is refused, and a rollback is pinned exactly to the journal snapshot.
  - Play is only allowed on the node holding the profile's world (OD-1, `WRONG_NODE`).
  - The store level rises to **53** on the first switch by a non-owner member. Build 52 then refuses the store, and
    the updater refuses to downgrade below 53.
- **Authoritative login** (`aurora.awf.multiprofile.login.authoritative: auto|true|false`, default `auto` =
  enabled when co-op is enabled, RESTART_REQUIRED).
  - The join restores the active profile's committed member head, not vanilla playerdata. The first entry into a
    co-op starts from an empty state. An unreadable head, a missing REQUIRED adapter or failed recovery refuses the
    join with a reason. There is no silent vanilla fallback.
  - **Lockstep autosave:** each time the server saves a player's vanilla playerdata (`PlayerSaveEvent`), the player
    is also captured and published to the profile. At most one save per player is pending.
  - **Session marker:** a per-account marker is opened at login and closed by the committed quit or shutdown save.
    After a crash, a refused save or the 52→53 upgrade, vanilla playerdata (`<level>/players/data/<uuid>.dat`)
    that is newer than the head is adopted once into the active profile. The adoption is applied only after it
    commits, and the marker is carried forward across generations. This is what prevents duplicated items or lost
    progress after a crash.
  - **Fail-closed:** a capture that cannot be saved (too large, REQUIRED adapter failure) freezes and disconnects
    the player. An operator can clear it with `/awf multiprofile session <player> reset-marker|clear-refusal`.
- **Adapter contract (RFC E03).**
  - New API types `ProfileDataCriticality`, `ProfileDataScope` and `ProfileAdapterDescriptor`;
    `ProfileStateAdapter.descriptor()` and `migrate()` (defaults keep Build 52 behaviour).
  - REQUIRED: a capture or apply failure refuses the switch. The adapter's criticality is stored with its data, so a
    missing REQUIRED adapter is refused with `ADAPTER_MISSING`.
  - BEST_EFFORT: a failure keeps the previously stored bytes. Unknown blobs are preserved, and exceeding the
    aggregate budget is refused rather than dropping data.
  - `aurora.awf.multiprofile.adapters.required` forces chosen adapters to REQUIRED. There are at most 31 plugin
    adapters.
- **Removing an online member** (leave or kick while the member is active in the co-op) captures the member, moves
  them to their next active profile or to a fresh solo profile, then removes them, as one recoverable flow. Offline
  members are re-pointed. `MEMBER_ONLINE` remains only when the member's session is on another node.
- **Quit snapshot at shutdown (PM-076).**
  - Feature patch 0081 changes the stop order. Before plugins are disabled, the state of every registered adapter
    is captured. After that, open containers are closed and every online player is captured and saved on the region
    shutdown thread.
  - All of this happens before the server saves vanilla playerdata, within
    `aurora.awf.multiprofile.shutdown.capture-deadline-ms` (default 10000, RESTART_REQUIRED).
  - Players not saved in time keep their pending intent and an open marker, so the next login recovers or adopts.
- **API.** New error codes are appended at the end: `ADAPTER_REQUIRED_FAILED`, `ADAPTER_MISSING`,
  `HEAD_UNREADABLE`. `saveProfile` now saves every member who is playing the profile on this node, and returns
  `PLAYER_OFFLINE` if nobody is.
- **CI fix carried in from Build 52 (PR #75).** The legacy bridge boot no longer races its own `cancelTasks` probe.

## Owner test cases

The test sources were written by agents and **never run by them**. CI runs them on push. They are
`CoopMemberPlayTest`, `CoopMemberPlayRedisTest` (skips without `redis-server`), `ProfileStateAdaptersTest`,
`ProfileShutdownHookTest`, `ProfileSettingsBuild53Test`, and the updated `CoopMembership*Test`. Case names follow
COOP-DESIGN §10 slice 5, plus cases for D1–D4 and E1–E4.

Manual cases:
- **TO-B53-01:** with co-op off, solo MultiProfile joins exactly as in Build 52.
- **TO-B53-02:** with co-op on, a member switches in, plays, and switches out, with two real clients.
- **TO-B53-03:** stop the server while players are online, then confirm their quit snapshots were written.
  `kill -9` mid-play, rejoin, and confirm the vanilla state is adopted with no duplication against chests.
- **TO-B53-04:** upgrade from 52 to 53 with players online and co-op on, and confirm the first join adopts vanilla
  playerdata.
- **TO-B53-05:** a REQUIRED adapter is missing (or fails) and the join or switch is refused. A BEST_EFFORT adapter
  fails and its previous data is kept.
- **TO-B53-06:** leave or kick an online member who is active in the co-op. No item is left in two places.
- **TO-B53-07:** a player whose capture is too large is frozen and kicked, and
  `/awf multiprofile session … clear-refusal` releases them.
- **TO-B53-08:** Redis variant of 02, 03 and 06, with two nodes and OD-1 refusals.

## Known limits

- Shutdown saves are still serial on the I/O lane, so a busy server may hit the capture deadline.
- A plugin adapter's `capture()` at shutdown is only logged when it is slow (over 500 ms); it cannot be bounded.
- Adoption decides by comparing the vanilla playerdata file's modification time with the head.
- Coming later: the bank, collections and salvage delivery (Build 54); audit, type rules and the per-account I/O
  queue (Build 55).
