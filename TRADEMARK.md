# SourbyCraft trademark policy

The SourbyCraft code is free software under the [GPL version 3](LICENSE.md). This policy covers
something the GPL does not: the names **SourbyCraft**, **Aurora** (as the SourbyCraft engine name),
**Aurora Nexus** and the other build codenames, and the SourbyCraft logos (together, "the marks"). It
is an additional term under section 7(e) of the GPL version 3, which permits declining to grant rights
to use trade names and trademarks.

## Allowed without asking

- Running unmodified official builds and saying your server runs SourbyCraft.
- Referring to SourbyCraft by name in articles, videos, reviews, plugin compatibility notes and
  bug reports.
- Saying that a fork or plugin is "based on SourbyCraft" or "compatible with SourbyCraft", as long as
  it is clear that it is not an official build.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Distributing a modified build, fork or repackaged jar under the SourbyCraft or Aurora name or logo,
  or under a name likely to be confused with it.
- Selling or offering paid access to anything presented as SourbyCraft, an official SourbyCraft
  build, or endorsed by SourbyCraft.
- Using the marks in a domain name, product name, server brand or logo in a way that suggests an
  official connection.

## Forks

The GPL lets you fork, modify and redistribute the code, including commercially. A fork must remove
the marks: rename the project, the jar and the in-game brand (`/version`, server brand, console
banner) and replace the logos. The GPL's own notices and the upstream copyright lines must stay.
